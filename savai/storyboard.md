Picture rules for this film (from CLAUDE.md + the user's notes):
- Narration says what was reported; **the picture never shows violence or wounds**. Injuries become objects on a blank page, numbers, closed doors, ghosts.
- Blood = hot pink. Things tied to the killers (their sneaker prints, the stolen jewellery, the broken coat-rack club) = hot pink. Nothing else is pink.
- **The three men are always the same: three identical figures filled solid in ink grey (먹색 `#3b3936`), no faces.** No one else in the film uses that colour (everyone else's hair is umber), so the three read at a glance as one unit, like shadows. They were never identified.
- Victims are drawn plainly and with dignity; no names on screen.
- The bar's exact address is not shown; maps stop at the Sinsa-dong level.
