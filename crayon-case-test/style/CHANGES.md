# Crayon Case — changes from Crayon Picture Book (crayon-book)

| # | Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | §2 Materials — paper | Warm off-white `#f4efe3`, clean | Yellowed `#dccca8`, uneven toning, 2–4 tide-ring stains, foxing, one fold crease, browned ragged edges; all anchored to the page | "A children's book that's been in a drawer for decades": the age itself feels wrong |
| 2 | §3 Colour logic | ~12 saturated crayons; one accent may echo the story | ~7 drab crayons (grey-brown, umber, ink grey, faded blue, olive-grey, ash) + **one accent, hot pink `#FF1493`**, only on key objects / perpetrator-linked things / clues; never blood. Wash = dirty tea-brown stain; resist wax is clear (paper-coloured) | A muted world makes the one pink thing impossible to miss; channel-wide accent rule |
| 3 | §2 Characters | Dot eyes with white catchlight, pink cheeks, expressive faces | Small dot eyes, no catchlight, no cheeks, no mouth; no drawn emotion; victims never cute | Quiet, unsettling, respectful to real victims; pink cheeks would break the accent rule |
| 4 | §5 Motion + §8 native moves | Lines draw in 0.4–0.9 s; lively substitution acting; boil full | Lines draw in 1.2–2.5 s; long holds; boil at half; **erasing** added as a material (ghost smear) and a native move, *Rubbed out* | Slow, deliberate pacing for documentary; erasing = the missing, the lied-about |
| 5 | §7 Sound | Ukulele, clarinet, toy piano, glockenspiel, flute; a simple lilt | Slow music box (winds down, can stop), low drone, sparse low notes; foley forward: crayon scratch, eraser, slow stain creep; no birdsong | Unease instead of lullaby |
| 6 | §4 Type & subtitles (channel rule) | Subtitles hand-drawn on the page (Patrick Hand), titles in Gaegu | Subtitles burned in afterwards: white Freesentation 4 Regular, bottom-centre, soft shadow; films rendered without them. Titles in crayon on the page in Freesentation 6 SemiBold | The channel uses one subtitle look across all its styles; English channel; user's font |

Kept: crayon wax-on-tooth physics, hand wobble and boil, picture-book composition and child perspective, indigo outlines (never black), occlusion/knock-out, wax resist (now clear wax under a stain), page-as-object moves.
