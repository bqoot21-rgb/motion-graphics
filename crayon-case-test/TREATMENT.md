# TREATMENT — Crayon Case demo: "Alder Street" (fictional)

A style test for Crayon Case: 29 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps, music and foley only (no voice, no subtitles). The case is invented: no real people, places or events.

## The idea

One page of a child's picture book records the night someone stopped being there. A woman is drawn in a window, then rubbed out; her window is coloured over. Then a spilled stain creeps across the lawn and shows what the page was hiding all along: footprints from the door to the gate, where one hot-pink glove is coloured in. Nothing violent is shown; the page only remembers.

## Style rows, where they show (CHANGES.md)

1. Aged paper: every frame; stains and the crease visible in the opening close and the held wide.
2. Drab box + one pink: the whole page is drab; pink appears once, at 23.6 s, on the glove only.
3. Blank faces: the woman in the window (9–11 s).
4. Slow drawing / erasing: the house (4–9 s), the erasing (11–14 s), boil at half throughout.
5. Sound: music box stopping (13.9 s), drone, crayon / eraser / stain foley.
6. Type: title and date in crayon, Freesentation 6 SemiBold; no subtitles in picture.

Native moves: clear-wax resist (the footprints), rubbed out, drawn live, colouring is an action (window → blue, glove → pink), boil (calming at the end), child logic (the date written over the page, a person in the window as big as the door).

## Different from the base demo

Structure (an investigation in three acts vs. a bedtime story), opening (a date written in close-up vs. a blank full page), signature shot (erasing a person vs. a wash over stars), camera path (close → house → window → wide → gate vs. moon → girl → sky → book), score shape (music box that stops and does not resolve vs. a lullaby heard in full), ending (a held clue vs. a pull back to a book and a page turn). 6 of 6 differ.
